# iPe (India Pay) — Merchant Onboarding

**Document ID:** ONB-POL-001
**Version:** 3.2
**Owner:** Merchant Risk
**Status:** Current

## 1. Purpose

This policy says what must be true before a shop can take payments through iPe (India Pay).

## 2. Required pack

A new merchant is not complete until all four items are on file:

1. Business registration
2. Government ID of the owner
3. Bank proof in the shop's legal name
4. A photo of the shopfront, taken within the last 90 days

If any item is missing, the merchant stays in review. An operator may not mark the pack complete.

## 3. Business type

Restaurants, grocery, and retail are allowed business types.

Money service, crypto, and gambling are not allowed. An operator cannot override this list.

## 4. Go-live

Only the Merchant Risk Lead may turn a merchant on, and only after the pack in section 2 is complete and the business type is allowed.

An operator may prepare the review. An operator may not turn a merchant on.
