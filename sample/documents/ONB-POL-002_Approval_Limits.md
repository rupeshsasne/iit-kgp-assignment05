# iPe (India Pay) — Approval Limits

**Document ID:** ONB-POL-002
**Version:** 1.4
**Owner:** Merchant Risk
**Status:** Current

Expected monthly volume is the shop's own estimate on the application.

| Role | May prepare a review | May turn a merchant on | Volume ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Operator | Yes | No | None. Operators do not activate. |
| Merchant Risk Lead | Yes | Yes | Up to ₹40,00,000 a month |
| Head of Payments | Yes | Yes | Above ₹40,00,000 a month |

If the estimate is above the lead's ceiling, the review goes to the Head of Payments. The assistant must not choose a lower volume to keep the decision with the lead.
